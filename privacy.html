StrumTune – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

StrumTune ("the app") is a guitar and ukulele tuner, metronome, chord library and practice app for Android. This policy explains what information is used when you use the app. There is no account and no login.

1. Microphone

The app uses the microphone only:

  • after you tap Start on the tuner, to measure the pitch of your instrument, and
  • while you use the practice recorder after tapping the record button.

Tuner audio is analysed in memory on your phone and immediately discarded. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Practice recordings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone; they leave your phone only if you choose to share them. The microphone is switched off when you tap Stop, leave the tuner, stop recording, put the app in the background, or play nothing for 3 minutes.

2. Information stored on your phone

The app remembers your settings (language, instrument, tuning, reference pitch, metronome presets), your practice minutes per day and a few counters (for example, so ads are not shown too often). This stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interactions (views and clicks) and diagnostics.

Google uses this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message and can change their choice any time in More › Settings › Ad privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Microphone audio is never shared with advertisers.

4. Online settings

The app may download a small public settings file (for example, how often ads may appear) from this website. No personal information is sent, apart from the normal technical data any web request includes (such as IP address).

5. Permissions

  • Microphone: tuner and practice recorder (see section 1).
  • Internet and network state: to load ads and the settings file.
  • Notifications: the playback notification (metronome with the screen locked) and the optional daily practice reminder.
  • Foreground service (media playback): so the metronome and backing tracks keep playing with the screen locked.
  • Vibration: the in-tune tick and the silent vibration metronome.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, photos, files or call logs.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Sounds and content

Instrument and drum sounds come from the FluidR3 GM SoundFont (MIT licence). Chord shapes come from chords-db (MIT licence). All songs in the songbook are traditional or in the public domain.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. For questions, contact the developer through the email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
